Kultur & Art Initiative e.V.

Culture is made together

We create spaces where art, learning and international exchange become shared experiences.

Founded in 2002

Creative practice connects people.

Kultur & Art Initiative e.V. is an intercultural and intergenerational non-profit organisation. Its members bring experience in youth work, refugee support, migration, non-formal education and media production.

01 · Culture

Art and cultural projects

Participatory formats that turn ideas, perspectives and lived experience into creative work.

02 · Learning

Creative and media education

Hands-on workshops where participants make, discuss and understand media together.

03 · Exchange

Youth mobility across borders

International encounters connect youth work, inclusion and collaborative learning.

04 · Locations

Hangar 21 and Antalya

Our offices support free extracurricular youth work, media education advice, workshops, performances and project development.

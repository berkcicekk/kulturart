About the project

Women Engagement Boost in Technology

Designed especially for young women and mothers with migrant backgrounds who want to strengthen their skills, confidence and professional opportunities.

By the end of the journey, you won't just have new knowledge. You'll have a roadmap to turn training into employment, confidence into leadership and ideas into action.
